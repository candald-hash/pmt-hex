We have scripts!

Provided by the amazing Saucekay and Deepish, there is now a scripted setup to the game, placing hexagons, Pokémon and even dealing Items, Trainer Cards and Starters to every player. Lots of thanks to Saucekay and Deepish for the brilliant work!

The next balance update (the Elite Four patch) is not ready yet, but they work FAST and I coudn't wait to release it. 

Still, I made some very small changes that were already good to go: A new trainer that can repel wild Pokémon, a new Event and a buff to a Super Event that has been criticized for a long time.
 
You can check the changelog below. Questions? Feedback? Requests? Message me on Steam or tweet @dcandal. Stay safe and thanks for playing.
