v 6.3 CHANGELOG

[GENERAL]
- The rulebook was fully redesigned and rewritten from scratch. I hope it has clearer, simpler instructions (and is prettier to look at).
- Added 3D Models for some Gen 8 Pokémon (many thanks to Multhor, check his models here: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2425249259).
- Added a new background ("Pokémon Center in the evening" by FristiStains).
- This update changes some type pairings that are used for balancing purposes. These changes reflect mainly on +4 Battle Items, Trainers and on the balanced roster. They were:
-- Rock and Fighting are no longer paired.
-- Normal + Steel pairings changed to Normal + Fairy.
-- Ice + Fairy pairings changed to Ice + Steel.
