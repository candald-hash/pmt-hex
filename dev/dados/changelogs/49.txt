v 4.9

[GAMEPLAY]
- Added an optional mechanic - Weakened Wild Pokémon (thanks user Diego Cruz/LeiGai for that suggestion): whenever someone fails to capture a wild pokémon, place a weakened token on it. When trying to capture weakened Pokémon you can add or subtract 1 to the dice roll (stackable with Poké-Ball Items). Pokémon can't have more than 1 weakened token on them. Legendary Pokémon do not get weakened.
