v 6.0 CHANGELOG

[GENERAL]
- Added Optional Content: "Achievement Mode".
- Changed the art for the +1 Token.
- Inactive Pokémon were reorganized to occupy less space.
- Some parts of the rulebook were rewritten or revisited for clarification, thanks to your feedback.
- You can travel from city to city directly now if you have already visited it (more on the rulebook).
- You can now use up to two Battle Items when battling Dual Gym Leaders.
- Victories in the Pokémon League now award 3 Item Cards (instead of 2).
- Now when stopping on the "Enter Pokémon League" space before taking one full lap on the hexagon you draw 1 Item Card (instead of losing your turn).

[HEXAGONS]
- Added B9 (Virbank, Leader Roxie). Tier 1.
- Added C11 (Driftveil, Leader Clay). Tier 2.
- Added D10 (Carnavale, Leader Byron). Tier 3.

[LEADERS]
- Added Roxie (B9, Poison, 8).
- Added Clay (C11, Ground, 17 Dual).
- Added Byron (D10, Steel, 14).

[OPTIONAL CONTENT]
- Added "Achievement Mode" (in this optional mode there are goals you can achieve and get different rewards - more info on the rulebook).
- Added 28 achievement cards for Achievement Mode.

[EVENTS]
- Removed "Extra Badge".

[ITEMS]
- Tweaked "Fly": No longer used to travel to cities. Using this Item must be your only action in your turn.
- Fixed "Rocket Attack" being tagged as a Quick Item instead of a Common Item.

[TRAINERS]
- Swapped Victor for Kahili (same bonuses: +1 to Flying Pokémon).
- Removed Brendan.
